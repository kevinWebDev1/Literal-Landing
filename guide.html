How to Use Literal

Welcome to the step-by-step guide for Literal—the #1 digital distraction avoider of 2026. This guide will walk you through setting up the unbreakable systems that make Literal superior to any other app on the market.

Why Literal Succeeds Where Others Fail

If you've tried other app blockers (like Apple Screen Time, Forest, or AppBlock), you know their biggest flaw: They are too easy to bypass. When the urge to doomscroll hits, you just click "Ignore Limit for 15 Minutes" or simply delete the app. Traditional blockers rely entirely on your willpower.

Literal is different. Literal does not rely on willpower. We use real-world accountability (the Keyholder system) and high stakes (Literal Tokens) to ensure you stay disciplined.

  • Competitors: Let you delete the app.
    Literal: Enforces Uninstall Protection. You cannot delete the app without your Keyholder's permission.
  • Competitors: YouTube is fully blocked, ruining studying.
    Literal: Features YouTube Study Mode, blocking brainrot Shorts while allowing educational videos.
  • Competitors: No social stakes.
    Literal: Forces you to ask a trusted friend for extra time. Izzat ka sawal hai (It's a matter of respect).

Step-by-Step Setup Tutorial

Prefer a video format? Watch the full video guide on our YouTube Channel.

1. Install & Initial Setup

Download Literal from the Google Play Store. Upon opening the app, you will be guided through the Initial Setup. You will need to grant Usage Access and Display Over Other Apps permissions. Unlike sketchy VPN blockers, Literal processes everything locally on your device ensuring 100% privacy.

2. Choose Your Fate: User vs. Keyholder

You will be asked to choose a role:

User: Choose this if you are the one who needs to focus and block distractions.
Keyholder: Choose this if you are managing someone else's rules (like a parent managing a child, or an accountability partner).

3. Pairing with a Keyholder

If you selected "User", you must pair your account with a trusted Keyholder. This is Literal's secret weapon. Generate a pairing code from your app and share it with your Keyholder. Once linked, they have the ultimate authority over your digital locks.

4. Navigating the Dashboard & Quotas

Go to your Dashboard to set your Daily Quota. Select the apps that distract you the most (Instagram, TikTok, etc.) and set a strict time limit. Once the timer runs out, the apps are hard-locked.

If you desperately need more time, you must send an Unlock Request to your Keyholder. If they deny it, you stay locked out.

5. Enabling Uninstall Protection & Study Mode

To make your discipline unbreakable, go to settings and turn on Uninstall Protection. Next, toggle on YouTube Study Mode to block the YouTube Shorts feed and only allow educational content. Finally, turn on Porn Addiction Mode for a strict, un-bypassable filter against adult websites.
